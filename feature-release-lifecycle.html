Feature Release Lifecycle

Proposal v0.4 · Sep 2026 Process view (BPMN) →
Flag-gated features on Conductor, from epic + flag through GA. Mobile-app releases follow their own beta path, out of scope here. The epic is the release: it walks the states we already use, with defined gates and owners. Dev tickets keep closing at merge; the epic is the only issue that waits, and its wait is the signal: merged is not live. No new tickets, no new pipeline, one new label (feature-release).
