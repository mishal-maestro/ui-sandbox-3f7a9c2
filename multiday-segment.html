What this argues

THE ONE IDEA

One container, four types. "DMC Booking" in the current design is not a DMC feature. It is a multi-day segment that happens to have been drawn for DMCs first. Rename it, let the type vary, and cruise stops being a special case.

THIS IS NOT A NEW PROPOSAL

Dustin already made this call in Conductor Squad Planning on 2026-07-21: one multi-day component type covering cruises, multi-day tours and multi-day train rides, because ancillary items can hang off those days. Cruise design was folded into DSN-32 with Juan in the same meeting. This prototype just makes that decision concrete enough to react to.

WHY CRUISE IS THE RIGHT TEST CASE

  • It is the hardest shape. 11 nights, 3 sea days, an overnight in port, two different countries, and a fare that is per person while the itinerary prices per package.
  • The supply side already models it. provider.schema.ts ships cruise_line and multiday_tour_operator as provider types today, while componentTypeSchema is still ['flight','hotel','experience','transfer','dining','other']. CON-1688 closes an asymmetry, it does not start from zero.
  • It is the highest-commission category we sell. Cruise 12.8% against a book average of 8.4% (Kenny Liao, #all-traveladvisors, 2026-06-09). That number appears on no cruise ticket.

BUILT FROM OPS' OWN FIELD LIST

The capture form is not invented. It is Nina Price's list from #ta-ops-pod-1 on 2026-03-19, four months before CON-1688 was written, and cited by no ticket:

  • Name of cruise ship, title of trip, dates
  • Embarkation date, city and time
  • Disembarkation date, city and time
  • Stateroom type and link
  • Total cost, experience style, perks

The demo sailing is real too: Christine's Celebrity Constellation booking from #cluster-rebeca on 2026-07-29, down to the booking ID and stateroom number.

WHAT THE CURRENT DESIGN CANNOT DO

  • Sea days. A day-grouped item list has nothing to show on Oct 14, 17 and 21. Kenny's ask on 2026-08-05 was that components be populated on every cruise day rather than left blank. Here they render as first-class.
  • The echo problem. The DMC package repeats its header on every day it covers. Two days is fine. Eleven is not. This uses one continuous segment with a day rail instead.
  • Per-person fare. The pricing toggle is Package or Per item. A cruise fare is per person, with port taxes on top. That is a third mode.
  • Port calls. Arrival and departure times per port are what shore excursions hang off. Rebeca asked for exactly this on 2026-08-04, to beat the cruise line's own excursions.
  • Not wiping the itinerary. Alejandro reported on 2026-03-19 that adding a cruise wiped the existing day-by-day. Never answered. The pre-cruise Rome night survives here on purpose.

THE FORK STILL OPEN ON CON-1688

A. One polymorphic multi_day_segment

Variants cruise / dmc / tour / rail. Shared span, day rail, pricing and linking. Type-specific attributes underneath. What this prototype shows.

B. Sibling component types

A cruise type, a DMC type, a tour type. Less abstraction now, four places to change later. CON-1834 (trains as their own type) is already pulling this way, and neither ticket links to the other.

QUESTIONS FOR DUSTIN

  1. Polymorphic multi_day_segment with a variant, or sibling types per shape? This is the open question on CON-1688 and it blocks CON-1693.
  2. Does CON-1834 (trains as their own component type) fold into this, or stay separate? Right now the two decisions contradict each other and neither ticket references the other.
  3. When DMC packages ship as ordinary components in v1 per CON-2193, what is the migration path once a real multi-day type lands? Nobody has raised this.
  4. Is per-person fare with separate port taxes in scope for v1 pricing, or does cruise inherit the package-price model and we absorb the error?
  5. Is cruise a must-have or a nice-to-have? CON-2169 (Redmond, Silversea) travels Aug 23 and Marco's read is that it expires before it can ship.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

Not a spec, not a design handoff, and not a data model. The schema shown is inferred from the Figma and from ops' Slack, not read from code. Juan owns the visual design on DSN-32. This is a conversation piece to unblock the CON-1688 decision.
