Demo trip: Bangkok (May 24 – 27) then Chiang Mai (May 27 – 29), with an arrival flight + transfer on May 24, an inter-city flight + transfer on May 27, and day-anchored dining / experiences in between.

Any collection date edit runs the same dependency scan; the prompt appears only when it affects other collections. Every affected collection is listed with its current date, the proposed date, and the dependency reason. Nothing moves silently.

Things to try inside the modal: nothing is ticked by default, so the confirm button reads "Move only [collection]" until you opt collections in · tick a row and watch the button, count, and trip banner update live · click any proposed date to type your own (row auto-ticks, "custom" pill + Reset appear, ranges keep their length) · "Shift all selected by N days" overrides every ticked row at once · conflict warnings are class-aware (items that belong inside the stay warn when left outside it; boundary and downstream items warn when they land inside it) · Select all / Clear for bulk ticking · Cancel, X, or Escape discards the edit entirely.
